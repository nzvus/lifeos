# ADR-0028: Exercises come with a written guide and personal targets from history

**Status:** accepted (the developer chose option A, 2026-10-02)
**Date:** 2026-10-02

## Context
After a real gym session the developer asked, for every exercise, for good
values (load, reps, sets, rest, warm-up), measured on them and on general
conventions, plus how to perform it and the most common mistakes. Today an
exercise has a name, a kind, a pattern and muscle groups, and the gym mode
shows only "last time".

## Options considered
### A. Guides written in the app, plus personal values computed from history
- Pros: the content is reviewed once and versioned with the code. The
  personal part is pure arithmetic on the user's own sets: the next load by
  double progression, an estimated 1RM, the working range. It needs no
  network and no key, and it can be tested.
- Cons: only the exercises with a guide get the written part, and the
  content has to be written.
### B. Guides generated by the assistant and stored
- Pros: covers any exercise.
- Cons: the quality varies, it needs a key, and it is hard to test. A wrong
  cue about form can hurt.
### C. A for the common exercises, B for the rest
- Pros: full coverage. Cons: two sources, two levels of trust.

## Decision
**A.**
- **Guides** live in `core/training/guides/`: one entry per common exercise.
  - A key, with aliases matched against the exercise's name (normalised:
    case, accents, punctuation).
  - The muscles worked, 3–5 cues for setup and execution, and 3–5 common
    mistakes.
  - Whether it needs a warm-up (compound movements: yes).
  - A default rep range and rest by role: compound 6–10 reps, 2–3 min;
    isolation 8–15 reps, 60–90 s.
  - Sources are the usual references (ACSM, NSCA, Schoenfeld's hypertrophy
    reviews). The text says it is guidance, not medical advice.
- **Personal targets** (pure, `core/training/targets.ts`), from the user's
  working sets:
  - **Double progression:** the range comes from the guide, or 8–12. If every
    working set last time reached the top of the range, the next load goes up
    one step (`loadStep`). Otherwise the load stays and the goal is one more
    rep.
  - An estimated 1RM (Epley), the best set, and the working range seen in the
    last sessions.
  - Bodyweight exercises progress by reps, cardio by minutes.
- **Where it shows:** in gym mode, a "Today: 3 × 10 × 42.5 kg" line and a
  "How to" panel per exercise; on the exercise page, the guide and the
  numbers.

Tripwire: if many of the exercises the developer uses have no guide after a
month, reconsider C.

## Consequences
- No migration: guides are code, and targets are computed on read.
- Exercises without a guide still get the personal numbers, with the 8–12
  default.
- Adding a guide is a code change with its test (aliases unique, ranges
  valid).
